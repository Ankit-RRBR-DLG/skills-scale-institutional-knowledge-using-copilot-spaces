# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Delivery Coordinators

### Role Summary
Delivery Coordinators keep day-to-day work, dependencies, and handoffs moving. They support the Project Manager by maintaining an accurate view of delivery progress without owning project scope or priority decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Track work against the agreed plan and surface blockers or dependency changes
- Coordinate handoffs between Developers and teams
- Keep delivery boards and action items current
- Prepare progress details for Project Manager status reporting

### Decision Rights
- May organize sequencing and handoffs within the agreed plan
- May not change scope, priorities, or delivery commitments; refer proposed changes to the Project Manager and Product Manager

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Work with the Project Manager to maintain the plan and escalate schedule or resource concerns
- Coordinate with Developers on blockers, estimates, and work status
- Confirm priority or acceptance questions with the Product Manager through the Project Manager

---

## Stakeholder Liaisons

### Role Summary
Stakeholder Liaisons make sure stakeholder needs, questions, and project updates reach the right people. They provide a reliable communication path but do not make product or delivery commitments on behalf of the team.

### Responsibilities
- Identify relevant stakeholders and their communication needs
- Gather questions, feedback, and concerns and route them to the accountable role
- Share approved status updates and record follow-up actions
- Flag conflicting expectations or emerging stakeholder concerns

### Decision Rights
- May choose appropriate channels and coordinate routine communications
- May not change product priorities, acceptance criteria, or project commitments; confirm these with the Product Manager or Project Manager

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Align messaging and escalation with the Project Manager
- Bring customer and business feedback to the Product Manager for prioritization and product decisions
- Route technical questions to Developers and relay responses without changing their meaning

---

## Risk Owners

### Role Summary
A Risk Owner is the named person accountable for monitoring and responding to a specific project risk. The role can be assigned to a Developer, Product Manager, Project Manager, or another qualified contributor; it is assigned per risk, not necessarily a separate team position.

### Responsibilities
- Assess the likelihood and impact of the assigned risk
- Define, track, and update mitigation and contingency actions
- Monitor triggers and report changes in exposure promptly
- Record outcomes and request help when mitigation is outside their authority

### Decision Rights
- May carry out agreed mitigations within their role and delegated authority
- May not accept significant residual risk or change project scope, budget, or commitments alone; escalate these decisions to the Project Manager and relevant decision-maker

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Keep the Project Manager informed so the project risk register and escalations stay current
- Consult Developers on technical risks and feasible mitigations
- Involve the Product Manager when a risk affects customer value, priorities, or acceptance criteria

## Release Coordinators

### Role Summary
Release Coordinators organize release readiness and execution so a completed change can move through the agreed release process safely and predictably.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate release dates, readiness checks, dependencies, and the release checklist
- Confirm that required testing, documentation, communications, and approvals are recorded
- Coordinate release-day handoffs and capture the outcome and follow-up actions
- Escalate readiness gaps, incidents, or schedule changes

### Decision Rights
- May coordinate release timing and operational steps within the approved plan
- May recommend proceeding, delaying, or rolling back based on readiness and agreed criteria
- May not waive required checks or independently authorize a release or rollback; follow the team's designated approval and incident process

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Coordinate with Developers on build quality, technical readiness, and deployment steps
- Work with the Project Manager on release dependencies, schedule, and stakeholder updates
- Confirm product acceptance or launch requirements with the Product Manager

---

## Retrospective Facilitators

### Role Summary
Retrospective Facilitators guide the team through a constructive review of its process and outcomes, helping turn observations into actionable improvements.

### Responsibilities
- Prepare and facilitate inclusive, blameless retrospective discussions
- Help the team identify patterns, root causes, and practical improvement actions
- Record agreed actions, owners, and follow-up dates
- Check whether previous improvement actions had the intended effect

### Decision Rights
- May select facilitation techniques and manage the discussion to ensure balanced participation
- May not assign blame or impose process changes; the team agrees actions, and the appropriate role approves changes that affect project commitments or policy

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Coordinate timing and follow-up with the Project Manager, who incorporates relevant actions into project tracking
- Invite Developers and other contributors to share delivery and technical learnings
- Include the Product Manager when improvements affect prioritization, customer feedback, or product ways of working
